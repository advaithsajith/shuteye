ShutEye

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Short version: ShutEye does not collect, send, sell or share any information about you or the pages you visit. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What ShutEye does on your device

ShutEye changes how web pages look in your browser. To do that it reads the colours and images of the page you are viewing. All of this happens on your device, and none of it is recorded or sent anywhere.

What ShutEye stores

ShutEye saves these things in your browser's own extension storage, on your device only:

  • your settings: theme, brightness, schedule and animation choices
  • the site rules you create, such as "never darken this site"
  • a list of sites that are already dark, so they are left alone next time
  • the page you last read in each PDF opened in the reader

Removing ShutEye deletes this data. It is never transmitted to the author or to anyone else.

Network requests

ShutEye makes no network requests of its own, with two exceptions that you start yourself:

  • To tell a photo from a diagram, ShutEye may load a second copy of an image that the page you are viewing already uses, from the same address the page used.
  • If you open a PDF by its link in the ShutEye reader, the reader downloads that PDF from the address you gave it. PDFs opened from your device are never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Read and change data on all websites: needed to restyle whichever page you open. ShutEye does not read form entries, passwords or page text for any other purpose.
  • Storage: needed to remember your settings.
  • Active tab: lets the control panel show the status of the page you are on.

This website

The ShutEye website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Like any web host, those services receive your IP address when you load the page. The author has no access to that information. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.
