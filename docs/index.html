Install

Not installed on this browser yet.

  1. Get Userscripts

    ShutEye runs inside Userscripts, an open-source Safari extension from the App Store. Install it and open it once.

    Open the App Store

  2. Switch it on

    Turn Userscripts on for Safari and allow it on all websites.

    Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions > Userscripts > Allow Extension, then All Websites > Allow.
    On iOS 17 or earlier: Settings > Safari > Extensions.

  3. Add ShutEye

    Tap the button. When the script opens, tap the page-menu button in Safari's address bar, choose Userscripts, and tap the install prompt.

    Install ShutEye

  4. Meet the fox

    Reload any website. It goes dark, and a small fox sits in the corner. Tap the fox to pick a theme, tune it, or switch a site off.

    No fox? Hold two fingers still on the page for a second.

Read PDFs

Safari's own PDF viewer cannot be restyled, so ShutEye has a reader of its own. Open a PDF from Files and it is drawn in your theme, on this device only.
Open the PDF reader

What you get

15 themesDracula, Monokai, Nord, Tokyo Night and more, or pick your own three colours.
Smart imagesPhotos stay natural. Diagrams, formulas and scanned pages go dark.
Site rulesAuto, always or never for each site. Dark sites are left alone.
MotionBlink, ripple, fade, curtain or mist when the lights go out.
ScheduleAlways, with the system appearance, or between set hours.
Fine-tuneBrightness, contrast and warmth for late reading.
